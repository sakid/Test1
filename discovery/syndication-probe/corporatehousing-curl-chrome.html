About this Rental

Blue is the New Green! Cyan/PDX is a beautiful place to live. Your apartment home is located in the city, yet surrounded by lush green spaces. A LEED Gold rated community, it has multiple, exquisite design features that reduce the amount of energy, water, and land consumed and with more clear and and a lower cost of living. Global influance meets local living at Cyan!

Cyan/PDX is located in Portland, Oregon in the 97201 zip code. This apartment community was built in 2009 and has 17 stories with 352 units.

The management and staff have always been very kind to me and have been prompt to address any service need I may have. I use the television lounge and the exercise facilities every day of the week, and have both worked on projects and meditated in the patio, adjacent to the Pettygrove Park that is beautiful every season.
