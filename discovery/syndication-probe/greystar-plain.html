Building overview

Blue is the new Green: Cyan/PDX is a beautiful place to live. Your apartment home is located in the city and yet, surrounded on 3 sides by lush green spaces. A LEED Gold rated community, it has multiple exquisite design features that reduce the amount of energy, water, and land consumed and with more clear air and a lower cost of living. Relax in our backyard, grill with your friends, or grab some fresh fruits and vegetables from our garden--This is living, the sustainable way. Global Influence meets local living at Cyan.
